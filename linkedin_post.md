I am happy to share my final Data Analytics internship portfolio.

Over the internship, I completed an end-to-end analytics project covering:

- Data cleaning and transformation
- Exploratory data analysis
- Business intelligence reporting
- Customer segmentation
- Interactive dashboarding
- Data storytelling
- Statistical hypothesis testing
- GitHub portfolio documentation

The main business insight from my project was that the company should focus on realized revenue recovery. The analysis showed a major gap between booked sales and completed revenue, mainly because of returns and cancellations.

For statistical validation, I tested whether online and offline sales channels had different delivery success rates. The result was not statistically significant, so the final recommendation was to focus on order quality and customer segment recovery rather than assuming channel was the main cause.

This project helped me understand how to move from raw data to business action.
