# LinkedIn Video Script - Task 5 Portfolio Walkthrough

Use this script for a 3-5 minute LinkedIn video.

## Script

Hello everyone, my name is Sandeep Saini, and this is my final portfolio walkthrough for my Data Analytics internship.

In this internship, I completed an end-to-end analytics project across five tasks. The goal was to start with raw data, clean it, analyze it, build insights, validate findings, and finally present everything as a professional portfolio.

In Task 1, I focused on data wrangling. I understood the dataset, checked data quality issues, cleaned the data, transformed fields, and prepared an analysis-ready file.

In Task 2, I worked on exploratory data analysis and business intelligence. I analyzed sales trends, category performance, channel performance, and customer behavior. I also created SQL-style business questions and summarized the findings in reports.

In Task 3, I performed a deeper analysis and created an interactive dashboard. I focused on customer segmentation and key business KPIs like realized sales, delivery rate, return or cancellation rate, and repeat customer behavior.

In Task 4, I converted the analysis into a business story and added statistical validation. I tested whether online and offline sales channels had different delivery success rates. The p-value was 0.592, so the difference was not statistically significant. This helped me avoid making a weak assumption and focus on the stronger business issue: revenue leakage from returns and cancellations.

The final business recommendation is that the company should focus on improving realized revenue. Instead of only increasing order volume, the business should reduce failed orders, protect champion customers, and launch recovery actions for at-risk customer segments.

For Task 5, I created this master GitHub portfolio. It includes links to all four previous repositories, the final refined presentation deck, key findings, technical skills demonstrated, and a simple portfolio website file.

Through this internship, I improved my skills in Python, data cleaning, EDA, SQL-style analysis, dashboard design, hypothesis testing, GitHub documentation, and business storytelling.

My biggest learning is that data analytics is not only about charts and numbers. It is about asking the right business question, validating the answer, and communicating the result clearly so that people can take action.

Thank you for watching my portfolio walkthrough.
